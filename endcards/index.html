How to use
  1. Pick a style and fill in the fields. The preview is exactly what downloads.
  2. Everything readable must stay inside the red title-safe box (the inner 80% of the frame). Single lines shrink to fit, down to the 40px on-air minimum; past that the tool asks you to shorten the copy instead.
  3. One accent per card. Tune-in copy renders in ALL CAPS in the accent; with TONIGHT, leave out "pm".
  4. Download HD for playout and UHD for the master. Both are PNGs named style, text, size.
  5. The support card's QR code is made from the web address. Upload your own only if you need a tracked code.
  6. Your last card is remembered in this browser.
