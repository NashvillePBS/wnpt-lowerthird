How to use
  1. Pick a sign and fill in the lines. The preview is exactly what prints.
  2. Letter fits an 8.5 × 11 sign holder; Tabloid is 11 × 17 for easels and doors. Switch orientation for wide spaces.
  3. Everything readable stays inside the dashed 0.5 in print margin; the headline shrinks only when a single word is too wide, and the tool tells you when to shorten the copy.
  4. Signs are white with a clear border all round, because office printers leave an unprinted edge and cannot print to the paper's edge: the accent band, and the Navy panel on the print-shop option, sit inside that border. Pick the band's color and thickness; Navy covers a lot of the page in ink.
  5. Partner logos: add the files under Partner logos and they line up above the station logo, each at its own shape; add more and the row wraps and the logos shrink. Keep the label plain: "Supported by".
  6. QR codes: the Donate and Our Apps signs carry the station's fixed, tracked codes, so every scan counts in Google Analytics. The custom sign makes a new wnpt.tv short link for whatever address you give it.
  7. The PDF is one page at the exact paper size, 300 dpi, no bleed or crop marks: print at 100% scale, never "fit to page".
  8. Your last sign is remembered in this browser.
